## Groups
Owner, admin, member. The same group permissions control is available during creation and from Group details. All members can initially add people, share a link/QR, send messages and edit ordinary group information. Each permission can be restricted to admins including the owner. Defaults are independent of group size. Only the owner/admins can change permissions. Admin approval defaults off. When on, only a direct addition by an ordinary member requires approval. Link/QR and owner/admin additions admit directly. Check existing groups before choosing a migration default.

## Channels
Owner/admins publish. Members view. Only the owner appoints/removes admins, deletes the channel or transfers ownership to an admin. The outgoing owner becomes an admin. The follower count is public. Staff can see each other. Channel surfaces and server responses must protect follower identities from staff and staff identities from followers and visitors. Only the sender of an invitation sees whether their contact explicitly followed through that invitation. Independent follows must not disclose that status.

One mobile-friendly, reusable SilverChat dialog with configurable steps. (1) Public-channel introduction covering visibility, privacy and responsibility, prohibiting explicit sexual content, drug sales, explicit violence and illegal content. (2) Name, existing generated/editable slug and availability check, optional description and optional photo with a prominent visual picker and preview. (3) Search/select contacts or skip. Create only on final confirmation or skip. Send invitations after creation, let recipients choose whether to follow, show failures and retry only those, preserving the channel.

## Communities
One owner. Community-wide roles apply to existing and future chats. Create General and official announcements together. General and new ordinary chats start open. Owner/admins can select owner only, owner + admins, owner + admins + moderators, or all. The official announcements chat allows owner/admins and individually granted moderators only. Owner/admins manage that exception. Removing the moderator role clears the grant. Migrate the one existing Avisos chat per existing community preserving messages. Persist official identity independently of its name. General and official announcements can be deleted. Later user-created chats use ordinary rules even with that name.

Owner/admins manage chats and community details including names/photos/descriptions. Moderators can edit descriptions. Only the owner can delete the community, appoint/remove admins and transfer ownership. Owner/admins appoint/remove moderators. Voluntary transfer leaves the outgoing owner as admin. On owner departure/account deletion, prefer admin, then moderator, then member, breaking ties by community seniority within role and preserving the existing deterministic fallback.
For expulsion, community-wide timed silence and deletion of someone else's messages, moderators act on ordinary members, admins on members/moderators, and the owner on all subordinate roles. Support 10 minutes, one hour, one day, one week and custom duration. Anyone currently authorized over the target can lift a silence. Expulsion blocks re-entry until owner/admin permits it. Rejoining gives ordinary membership. Optional expulsion reason and unchecked option to delete this person's messages across the community. Include a blocked list, confirmations for destructive actions and ownership transfer, and a staff-readable audit log covering roles, silences/lifts, expulsions, publishing permissions and transfers with actor, time and optional reason.
