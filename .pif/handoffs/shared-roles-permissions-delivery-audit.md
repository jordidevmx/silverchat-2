## Findings verified by source inspection

1. Channel identity privacy. The new migration defines is_channel_staff(channel, user) with elevated read access and grants authenticated execution (20261120194500_shared_roles_permissions.sql:350, 2667-2701). The boolean result describes the supplied user, even when distinct from the caller. Existing authenticated owner/follower predicates in 0002 also accept a subject ID; the 0004 MFA wrapper does not restrict the subject to the caller. The promised privacy boundary must cover the callable functions as well as the views. Restrict externally callable predicates to authorized subjects and test authorization for other identities with distinct users.
2. Participant counting. The staff restriction in unfollow_channel coexists with the earlier direct own-row DELETE policy/grant on channel_followers (0002:426, 698-699); the new migration replaces the SELECT policy only. Enforce the staff-follower invariant across table writes as well as RPCs and demonstrate count consistency after attempted staff unfollow.
3. Channel invitations. ChannelView still mounts ChatGrowthHost, which routes to ChannelInviteAdminModal for the empty-channel card. That modal searches global users and sends a personal DM naming the sender as inviting staff (ChannelInviteAdminModal.tsx:47 onward). Route this entry point through the approved contact-only, channel-attributed flow; verify every invitation entry point.
4. Partial invitation delivery. send_channel_invitations returns per-recipient successes and failures within successful responses. useCreateChannelDialog.ts:120 checks only the outer error and marks every recipient sent, clears failures and exits. ChannelInvitationsSection.tsx:161 likewise ignores the individual result. Preserve the channel, show per-contact status and retry only failed contacts. Add a mixed-result acceptance check, including the actual response shape.
5. Groups and communities. GroupInviteSection.tsx offers a token link and copy/reset/revoke controls but no QR rendering. CommunityModerationModals.tsx:36 offers 15 minutes and three longer fixed durations; the agreed 10 minutes and custom duration are missing. permissions.ts:178 grants the client capability for managing community admins to admins too; the modal displays Promote admin to them although the server correctly reserves it to the owner. Align UI and database and cover the real controls in tests.
